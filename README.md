# 💫 About Me

<img align="right" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/WUlplcMpOCEmTGBtBW/giphy.gif" width="200">

Hey there! I'm **Shurjeel Khan** — a builder who loves bringing ideas to life, on-chain and beyond.

I started in **Web3**, building smart contracts and on-chain systems with Solidity and Foundry. What pulled me in? **Decentralization.** The idea that we can build a safer, fairer, more transparent world — that mission hasn't changed; it's just expanded.

Right now, I'm deep in **AI** — working through CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python, and shipping what I learn as real, working projects. Search algorithms, game-playing AI, knowledge-based reasoning, practical tools people can actually use.

My mission is simple: **build things that matter**, through code, curiosity, and consistent shipping.
